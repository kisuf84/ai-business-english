§ 03 · The Author

Brice Gadou

Business English & Customer Service Coach

Brice Gadou is a business English specialist dedicated to helping support professionals think clearly under pressure in a second language. Mr. Gad reflects his belief that real fluency in customer service isn't reciting phrases — it's reading a situation correctly and choosing the right response, in the moment.

Designed for B1–B2 learners who already work in, or aspire to work in, customer-facing roles in English — Mr. Gad Customer Service is more than a language course. It's a working rehearsal for the customer moments you'll actually face.
