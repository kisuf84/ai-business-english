Instant Feedback

Know Exactly What to Improve.

Every time you speak, Aria scores four dimensions that actually predict how you come across — not just whether you used the right verb tense.

Tone

How natural and confident you sound, not just what you said.

Fluency

Pacing, hesitation, and how smoothly your ideas connect.

Grammar

Structure and accuracy, flagged clearly, not just marked wrong.

Vocabulary

Whether your word choice matched the situation.
