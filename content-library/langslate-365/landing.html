Questions

Everything you're wondering

Do I need existing business experience?›

No. Level I starts with genuine basics — introductions, small talk, email — and builds from there. If you're already comfortable, jump straight to a higher level's opening lessons and see where you land.

What happens if I miss a day?›

Nothing bad. Your streak resets, but every lesson stays unlocked and waiting for you — there's no penalty beyond a broken streak counter.

Does the AI speaking feedback need an internet connection?›

Live transcription needs a connection, since your browser's speech engine processes it. But the actual scoring — tone, fluency, grammar, vocabulary — runs locally and instantly, with no external API call and nothing uploaded.

Can I start at a harder level?›

Yes. You can preview a higher level's opening lessons to honestly test where you land before committing to a plan.

Which browser works best for speaking practice?›

Chrome or Edge, on desktop or Android, give the most reliable live transcription. Other browsers may work with reduced accuracy.

Is this a subscription that auto-renews?›

No. Every plan is a single one-time payment for that period — no auto-renewal, no surprise charges. You decide when, and if, to continue.
