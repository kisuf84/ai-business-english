    § 02 · The Method

    Designed to train, test, and transform.

    ETHOS is built on the conviction that customer service excellence is a strategic capability — not a soft skill. Every module pairs business-English fluency with real-world judgment, technical literacy, and the ethical clarity that defines premium service.

    01

    Premium Curriculum

    One hundred modules covering every domain of modern customer service — from the foundations of empathy and active listening to AI-augmented operations and global service strategy.

    02

    Realistic Scenarios

    Every MCQ is rooted in plausible workplace situations — complaint handling, omnichannel coordination, escalation, retention, recovery — written for international business professionals.

    03

    Automatic Grading

    Instant scoring with tiered performance feedback — from foundational to executive fluency — plus the vocabulary and strategic insight earned from each module.

    04

    Progress That Persists

    Your completion and scores are remembered across sessions. Return any time to revisit your strongest topics, or to retake a module and refine your fluency.
