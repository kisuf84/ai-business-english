Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ›

Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from your account settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

What's included in the 12-month plan? ›

The 12-month plan gives you full access to all 500 lessons, the complete Word Bank, all exercises, and every new lesson added during your subscription — at the lowest monthly rate available.

Do I need to be online to study? ›

An internet connection is required to access lessons and stream audio. We are working on an offline mode for a future update.

Which level should I start at? ›

Browse the lesson previews for each level and choose the one that feels like a slight challenge but still understandable. A1 is for complete beginners; C1 is for advanced speakers who want to reach near-native fluency.
